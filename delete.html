資料刪除說明

Capeat 照吃（開發者：Winmoon）｜最後更新：2026 年 10 月 25 日

重點：Capeat 照吃沒有帳號、沒有伺服器，我們（開發者 Winmoon）不持有你的任何資料。你的資料只存在你自己的手機裡，由你自己在 App 內刪除，不需要向我們提出申請。

如何刪除你的資料

  1. 食物諮詢歷史紀錄：打開 App →「食物諮詢」→「歷史紀錄」→ 點開某一筆按「刪除這筆」，或按「全部清除」。這會一併刪除對應的文字、縮圖與你寫的「AI 更正回覆」。
  2. 健康資料（病史、完整健檢、簡易健檢、InBody）：打開「健康資料」→ 到對應的分頁，刪除單筆紀錄，或清除範本；病史、過敏、用藥類別、近期狀況與生活習慣可直接改成空白。
  3. API Key：「設定」→「AI 設定」→ 選到該服務 →「清除」。
  4. 一次全部刪除：在手機的系統設定中解除安裝 Capeat 照吃，或到「設定 → 應用程式 → Capeat → 儲存空間 → 清除資料」。App 內加密儲存的全部資料（病史、健檢、歷史紀錄與縮圖、API Key、已記住的備份密碼）會一併刪除。

會刪除與不會刪除的資料

  • 會刪除：App 內的病史與生活習慣、健檢與 InBody 紀錄與範本、AI 分析快取、食物諮詢與標籤歷史（含縮圖與更正）、API Key、已記住的備份密碼。
  • 不會自動刪除：你自己另存的備份檔（請到你存放的位置，例如手機檔案或雲端空間自行刪除），以及拍照時存進手機相簿的照片（Pictures/Capeat 或 Pictures/Foodref 資料夾，請到相簿刪除）。
  • 已送給第三方 AI 服務的資料：使用 AI 功能時，你選擇的服務（Google Gemini、Anthropic Claude 或 OpenAI ChatGPT）會收到你送出的內容。我們無法代為刪除；請依該服務的隱私權政策與帳號設定，向其申請查詢或刪除。

保留期限

我們不保留任何你的資料，因此沒有保留期限。App 內資料會一直存在你的手機，直到你刪除或解除安裝為止。

Data Deletion

Capeat (developer: Winmoon) · Last updated: October 25, 2026

In short: Capeat has no accounts and no servers, and we (the developer, Winmoon) hold none of your data. Your data exists only on your own phone, so you delete it yourself inside the App; you do not need to send us a request.

How to delete your data

  1. Food consultation history: open the App → "Food Consult" → "History" → open an entry and tap "Delete this entry", or tap "Clear all". This also deletes the related text, thumbnail, and any correction you wrote.
  2. Health data (medical history, comprehensive checkup, simple checkup, InBody): open "Health Data" → go to the relevant tab, delete individual records, or clear the template; your medical history, allergies, medication classes, recent conditions, and lifestyle habits can simply be emptied.
  3. API key: "Settings" → "AI settings" → select the provider → "Clear".
  4. Delete everything at once: uninstall Capeat in your phone's system settings, or go to Settings → Apps → Capeat → Storage → Clear data. All data stored encrypted in the App (medical history, checkups, history and thumbnails, API keys, and any remembered backup password) is deleted.

What is and is not deleted

  • Deleted: medical history and lifestyle data, checkup and InBody records and templates, AI analysis cache, food and label history (including thumbnails and corrections), API keys, and any remembered backup password.
  • Not deleted automatically: backup files you saved yourself (delete them where you stored them, for example in your phone's files or cloud storage), and photos saved to your phone's gallery when taking pictures (the Pictures/Capeat or Pictures/Foodref folder; delete them in your gallery).
  • Data already sent to a third-party AI service: when you use AI features, the service you chose (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or OpenAI ChatGPT) receives the content you send. We cannot delete it for you; please follow that service's privacy policy and account settings to request access or deletion.

Retention

We keep none of your data, so there is no retention period. Data inside the App stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the App.

Contact
